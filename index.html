The Challenge

FastSigns sells far more than signs — compliance signage, installation, surveying and permitting, graphic design, point-of-purchase displays, and interactive digital signage, all through a network of 650+ independently owned locations. The site's content had grown service by service, with no shared classification tying it together. I led the information architecture and UX for a full rebuild: a content inventory, a migration strategy, and one taxonomy built around what customers were actually trying to solve.

My Role

Organizing every service into one system.

Lead Information Architect (IA) User Experience (UX) Designer

From the first content audit to the final sitemaps, I owned the taxonomy strategy that tied every service into one findable structure.

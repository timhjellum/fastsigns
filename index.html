The Challenge

FastSigns sells far more than signs — compliance signage, installation, surveying and permitting, graphic design, point-of-purchase displays, and interactive digital signage, all through a network of 700+ independently owned locations. The site's content had grown service by service, with no shared classification tying it together. I led the information architecture and UX for a full rebuild: a content inventory, a migration strategy, and one taxonomy built around what customers were actually trying to solve.
